* All builds (*except* DOS 16‑bit), support ***all*** the optional methods:
  * BWT, bzip2, CMPSC, LPAQ8, LZFSE, LZMA, PPMd, WavPack, WZ‑JPEG, WZ‑MP3, XZ/LZMA2, Zopfli, and Zstandard.
* DOS 16-bit builds do not include `zipopt` and support a subset of all compression and decompression methods:
  * Compression: Store, DCL‑Implode, DEFLATE, Shrink, Reduce, and ZIP‑Implode
  * Decompression: Store, DCL‑Implode, DEFLATE, Deflate64™, Shrink, Reduce, and ZIP‑Implode
* It is *highly* recommend that DOS users with a 386 or higher processor use the [DOS 386 build](https://github.com/johnsonjh/infozip-av/releases/download/v0/dos386.exe).
