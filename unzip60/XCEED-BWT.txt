Xceed has a proprietary, BZip2-derived BWT streams stored under ZIP
compression method 18.

Method 18 is also assigned to IBM TERSE sadly.

Support for Xceed BWT does not implement IBM TERSE!

Xceed bitstream framing:

* First byte: ASCII digit '1' through '9' selecting the maximum BWT block
  size in units of 100,000 bytes.
* Bit-packed 0x31 marker and block payload for each BWT block.
* Bit-packed 0x17 end marker, then zero bits through the last byte.

The ordinary BZip2 48-bit block markers and per-block CRCs are absent.

The extractor accepts BWT only after successfully decoding an entire
stream and valid size and CRC-32 check.

Failure cannot not establish whether the member is IBM TERSE or
malformed data, at least not until we get IBM TERSE test files.

ZIP member LISTING thus uses a deliberately ambiguous description.

Decoding allocates up to 4.5MB of memory to work.

Future TERSE support will need a separatelyt validated TERSE decoder
and explicit disambiguation rules
